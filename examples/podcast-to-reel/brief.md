**Source:** "Mystery Bounty Is a Completely Different Game", GTO LAB podcast (YouTube 17QO6NohJ8E, 36 min). Speakers: Nick Petrangelo, Daniel Dvoress, Axel Hallay.

## Topic and story
**"It's not 'adjust a little', it's a completely different game."**
1. **Hook (Nick):** "I'm in a bounty, I'll adjust a little bit… call wider… play looser. And really the answer is you're playing a completely different game."
2. **Example (Dan → Axel → Nick):** the hijack jams 11bb. What does the big blind call? "Any two." The solver screen shows the BB calling 95.7% of hands.
3. **Why (Nick, over the solver EV tabs):** plenty of hands lose in prize-pool EV ($ppEV, red). The knockout EV ($koEV, green) makes them profitable. Added together, they beat folding ($EV).
4. **The mistake (Axel + Nick):** players stay too tight at this stage and miss out on money they could pick right up off the table.
5. **Takeaway (Dan):** know which factors to focus on so you can switch gears faster than your opponents.
6. **End card:** the GTO LAB logo lands on the original sting, then "Full episode on YouTube".
